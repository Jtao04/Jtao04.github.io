My Interests & Projects

One of my biggest interests is data visualization. I love the challenge of taking a complex dataset and transforming it into something visually clear and intuitive. Tools like D3.js, Python's Matplotlib, and Tableau have become central to how I approach analysis and presentation. I believe that a well-designed chart can communicate more effectively than a paragraph of text.

Beyond visualization, I am deeply interested in machine learning. I enjoy working on classification and regression problems, exploring how different algorithms behave on real-world data. I have worked on projects ranging from predicting housing prices to analyzing sentiment in social media posts. Each project teaches me something new about both the data and the methods used to interpret it.

I am also passionate about open-source collaboration and believe strongly in sharing knowledge with the broader community.
